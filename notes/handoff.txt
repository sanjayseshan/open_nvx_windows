## 3DMark performance and failure evidence
- Latest kernel sample: 212,992 submitted / 212,983 completed batches (9 outstanding), 37,504,871 dwords, 3,414,425 BO lookup-slot scans, 531,465 fence-wait calls, and 64,132 one-millisecond wait polls. This is a heavy stream of small submissions; most batches retire promptly, with only a small queue lag.
- User reported about 3 FPS. Kernel counters alone do not prove whether CPU submission, shader/GPU execution, or synchronization dominates.
- The extracted Nouveau trace has repeated `kernel rejected pushbuf: Operation not permitted` messages and one `NVC0: miptree BO allocation failed`. These are concrete errors to trace; they may explain retries or lost work, but the current log does not link them to a GPU crash.
- The planned userland `NOUVEAU_*_PROFILE` summaries were not emitted, so timing is still missing for translation, winsys/ring wait, miniport escape, and shadow uploads. Keep this profiling gap open.
