## Fix in 3.0.0.324
- Root cause was per-draw retirement overhead: each referenced BO scanned all 32 pending-copy slots and took the global buffer lock even when the BO had no pending presentation copy.
- Added a BO-level `CopyRefs` fast path and an atomic slot-state check before locking. Async presentation remains enabled.
