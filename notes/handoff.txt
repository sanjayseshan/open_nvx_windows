- New concrete failure: current run logs `NVX [GART]: MmAllocatePagesForMdl

  while the channel is not faulted. The Win98 shim
  `src_win98/shim/nvx_mdl_win98.c` implements `NvxMdlAllocateGartBacking` by
  calling `VideoPortAllocateCommonBuffer`, then fabricating a sequential PFN
  array. That requires physically contiguous backing and is not equivalent to
  XP's `MmAllocatePagesForMdl`; fragmented Win98 physical memory can reject
  these medium/large BOs. This is now the strongest log-backed explanation for
  missing textures/render targets and the black scene.
- The top-left-only flashing may also involve Nine's present rectangle or
  viewport scaling. Investigate after fixing backing allocation; do not assume
  that the BO table change fixes presentation.
