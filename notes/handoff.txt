## Highest-value next work

1. Attribute fence waits by caller and record elapsed cycles/time, distinguishing map/readback, dirty upload, push-ring reuse, pending-copy reuse, and pending-batch pressure. Current aggregate wait counters are ambiguous.
2. Check if full shadow readbacks are on the frame path. `NvxNouveauBoRefreshRead` downloads a full BO and merges it word-by-word; instrument bytes and duration before changing semantics.
3. Profile the guest render thread / NVC0 client path. Current miniport counters indicate low average per-submit CPU work; likely cost is in Nine/Gallium command generation or actual GPU workload. Aggregate CPU can hide one busy thread on six vCPUs.
4. Consider batching more work per kick only after the profile shows submission-bound frames. Preserve packet and BO ownership validation.
5. Treat CSMT as a separate, opt-in investigation. The NVC0 persistent-coherent capability is currently not established; falsely advertising it risks races/corruption.
