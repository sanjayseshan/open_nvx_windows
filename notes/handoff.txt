## Likely performance leads

- QEMU register snapshots repeatedly caught active guest CPUs 1 and 3 at `RIP=fffff8000081043a`, `CPL=0`, with `RDX=0x608`. The preceding instruction is `inl %dx,%eax`, consistent with reading QEMU's emulated ACPI PM timer. This is a concrete lead for expensive high-resolution timer queries/VM exits, but the snapshot has no caller stack and does not prove which process is polling it.
- The pinned Wine submodule is Wine 1.7.55 (`a786dca`). Its `wined3d_cs_create()` chooses `wined3d_cs_st_ops`; command handlers execute synchronously on the caller. This build does not have a CSMT worker-thread implementation or a `csmt` setting. That can serialize D3D state translation and GL submission, and is a separate likely contributor.
- Neither lead is confirmed as the sole cause. GT2 has an unusually large workload, and the short smoke tests do not reproduce its D3D shader/draw pattern.
