## Corruption root cause and fix

Nine was built without `HAVE_FUNC_ATTRIBUTE_PACKED`, while the Mesa ICD used it. Consequently `enum mesa_prim` was 4 bytes in Nine and 1 byte in the ICD; `pipe_draw_info.index_size` was at offset 4 vs offset 2. Equal overall struct sizes concealed the ABI mismatch, and indexed draws were read with the wrong fields. Cross-compiled layout probes confirmed the mismatch and the corrected layout. The .26 user visual pass confirms the fix on hardware.

- `build_d3d9_nine.sh` loads `patches/mesa9x/nvx-nine-build.mk`, forces the packed-enum define, and invalidates cached generated objects when ABI flags change.
- `mesa_patches/nine/mesa99.c` has compile-time ABI assertions.
- `patches/mesa9x/0007-xp-crt-failure-termination.patch` keeps the clean Nine rebuild XP-compatible; the apply script handles nested winpthreads.
- `patches/mesa9x/0005-nine-draw-range-trace.patch` and `0006-nine-draw-range-metadata.patch` are external Nine diagnostics and are applied by `test/apply_mesa9x_25_patches.sh`.

## Performance status

The .26 live counters show many small submissions: about 37,600 TSC cycles per miniport submit IOCTL, including ~10,500 cycles validating each stream and ~1,160 cycles of buffer lookup. Roughly 0.8 timed fence waits and 7 poll iterations per wait are recorded. This indicates avoidable CPU submission overhead, but these counters do not measure GPU utilization or frame duration, so they do not yet explain the full graphics slowdown.

The reported 1870 score is consistent with the reported game-test rates: 3DMark05 computes the overall score from the geometric mean of GT1/GT2/GT3 FPS (250 × cube root of their product), giving ~1,842 for 10/4/10 FPS. CPU tests are separate and excluded from that score; Futuremark’s white paper says they force software vertex processing to measure CPU performance. Their ~1 FPS result therefore points at the guest CPU/virtualized software-vertex workload, not the RTX 3070 path.

Next: profile per-frame time and batches for each game test, and separate Nine command generation, miniport submit/validation, fence waits, and GPU execution. Compare one run with diagnostic logging disabled. Do not infer GPU saturation from successful batch completion alone.

`NVX_AUTO_RETRY=1 NVX_RUN_3DMARK=1 NVX_D3D9_BACKEND=nine NVX_NINE_DEBUG=1 NVX_NINE_ONLY=0 ./test/test_full_driver.sh`
